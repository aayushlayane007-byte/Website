01  /  Innovation

Innovation Challenge 1 — UC MarketHub

During Innovation Challenge 1, I worked with three classmates to pitch UC MarketHub, a student-focused marketplace for textbooks, lab gear, and dorm items. We started by talking to friends and clubmates about how much unused stuff piles up at semester's end, then drafted a simple problem and solution slide to confirm the pain point.

My main jobs were outlining the core features, including verified UC email login, quick photo listing, and a rating system, and turning those ideas into a rough Figma mock-up for our final pitch. The challenge taught me the fundamentals of design thinking, scoping an MVP, gathering quick feedback, and translating that into a clear story for judges.

Presenting the deck also gave me a first real taste of public speaking in front of faculty and peers. Most importantly, it showed how engineering ideas intersect with business viability and pushed me to think more deeply about entrepreneurship and product design.

02  /  Honors Experience

Exploring Culture, Architecture and Urban Life in Chicago

During my self-designed Honors experience, I traveled to Chicago to explore how architecture, culture, and public spaces shape the identity of a major city. As one of the most influential urban centers in the United States, Chicago provided an opportunity to study how engineering, design, and cultural diversity contribute to the character and function of a modern city.

Over five days, I visited locations including Navy Pier, Millennium Park, the Magnificent Mile, the Griffin Museum of Science and Industry, Adler Planetarium, and the University of Chicago. I also explored cultural environments such as Chinatown, the German Christmas Market, and the Thanksgiving Parade. Through structured observation, journaling, and photography, I examined how architecture, infrastructure, and community spaces influence daily life and create a sense of place.

One of the most memorable aspects of the experience was viewing the Chicago skyline from 360 Chicago and participating in the architecture river tour. These experiences highlighted how the city combines innovation, engineering, and design to address environmental and urban challenges. From iconic skyscrapers to the Chicago Loop and riverfront, I gained a deeper appreciation for how architecture can reflect both the history and ambitions of a city.

This experience strengthened my understanding of urban systems, cultural diversity, and the relationship between engineering and the built environment. It also encouraged me to think more critically about how cities are designed and how public spaces shape the experiences of the people who use them. Through this experience, I developed a greater appreciation for the complexity of urban life and the role architecture plays in shaping community identity.

03  /  Leadership & Education

ENED 1120 Teaching Assistant

As a Peer Teaching Assistant for ENED 1120: Engineering Design Thinking at the University of Cincinnati, I supported first-year engineering students as they developed problem-solving, teamwork, and project-based engineering skills. My role included helping students with homework, general problem-solving, project guidance, grading, and exam preparation. While I mostly assisted the instructor and other TAs, I also had opportunities to lead students through problems and support them during review sessions.

This experience impacted me because it showed me that teaching requires more than knowing the material. I had to explain concepts in different ways depending on each student's level of understanding. Sometimes that meant breaking down a physics or calculus-based problem step by step, and other times it meant helping a team think through their project more clearly. Through this role, I became more confident in my own knowledge and more comfortable communicating in front of others.

This experience shaped my definition of a Global Citizen Scholar by showing me the importance of helping others succeed. Engineering students come from different backgrounds, learning styles, and confidence levels, and being a TA helped me better understand how to support people where they are. To me, being a Global Citizen Scholar means using what I know to contribute to a community and help create an environment where others can learn, grow, and feel supported.

04  /  Honors Experience

Exploring Culture, Architecture and Urban Life in Miami

During my self-designed Honors experience, I traveled to Miami to explore how culture, architecture, and environment shape the identity of a major coastal city. Building on my previous experience in Chicago, I wanted to understand how a city influenced by tourism, climate, and Latin American culture develops a unique character through its neighborhoods, public spaces, and urban design.

Over seven days, I visited locations including Wynwood Walls, Little Havana, Vizcaya Museum and Gardens, the Miami Design District, Downtown Miami, Brickell, and Key West. I observed how architecture, public art, and cultural traditions contribute to the identity of different communities. Through structured observation, journaling, and photography, I studied how people interact with urban spaces and how environment influences city life.

One of the most memorable aspects of the experience was exploring Key West and traveling through the Florida Keys. I also gained a deeper appreciation for Miami's cultural diversity, particularly the influence of Cuban and Latin American communities visible through language, food, art, and everyday life. Locations such as Wynwood and Little Havana demonstrated how culture can shape neighborhoods and create a strong sense of identity.

This experience strengthened my cultural awareness and broadened my understanding of how cities develop in different ways. Comparing Miami to Chicago helped me recognize that architecture, environment, and culture all play important roles in shaping urban life. It also increased my independence and confidence by challenging me to navigate new environments, adapt to unfamiliar situations, and engage more thoughtfully with the communities around me.
